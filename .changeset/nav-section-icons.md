Eight icons for the sections of a navigation tree (D137), for the `icon` slot of `NavItem` and `NavGroup`: `IconShieldCheck`, `IconBlocks`, `IconFileCog`, `IconCrosshair`, `IconCirclePlay`, `IconChartColumnBig`, `IconScrollText` and `IconActivity`. Each is Lucide's glyph of the same name, copied from lucide-static 1.75.0, and has the shape of every other Psi icon: a 24-unit grid, a 2px round stroke in `currentColor`, `size` defaulting to 20, and `aria-hidden="true"`.

The package now ships `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`. It carries Lucide's ISC licence and Feather's MIT licence, which their icons require in every copy, and lists which Psi icons come from each. 24 of Psi's earlier icons are Feather's, and the package had never carried Feather's notice; it does now. The three brand marks come from Simple Icons (CC0).
